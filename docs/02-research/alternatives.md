# Alternatives

What people use today when a claim goes wrong, what each covers and where it stops. Evidence IDs refer to the [evidence register](evidence-register.md); source IDs to [sources](sources.md).

## Comparison

| ID | Alternative | Who does the work | What it covers | Where it stops | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-01 | Manual workaround: email threads, re-uploads, scans of everything, insurer grievance team in copy, then Bima Bharosa and the Ombudsman | The claimant or a family member | Everything, if the person is persistent and knows the rules | Each step restarts with a new handler; no shared record of what was sent; escalation windows missed; months of effort during illness | E-06, E-07, E-09, E-10 |
| A-02 | Insurance agent or advisor | The person who sold the policy, or an advisor service | Advice before purchase; sometimes help at claim time | Help depends on the individual; agents in E-06 and E-09 were unreachable or unhelpful | E-06, E-09, E-11; S-28 (vendor) |
| A-03 | Broker or intermediary claim assistance | Staff of the intermediary | Filing help, follow-ups, complaint drafting and Ombudsman preparation, according to the vendor | Users disagree on whether help continues after sale (C-01 replies); eligibility and fees not stated publicly; human capacity | S-25 (vendor); C-01 replies |
| A-04 | Hospital insurance desk or TPA desk | Hospital staff | Cashless coordination during the stay | Ends at discharge; does not follow reimbursement, disputes or payment; the hospital may not disclose the approved amount (RF-16) | E-01, E-11, RF-16 |
| A-05 | General-purpose chatbot | The claimant, with a chatbot | Reading policy terms, checking a discharge summary, drafting emails and complaints | No memory of the case across weeks, no deadlines, no record of submissions, no reconciliation of amounts; can be confidently wrong about rules | E-09 and its replies |
| A-06 | Lawyer or consumer forum | A lawyer, or the claimant in person | Formal disputes and compensation | Cost and time; the last step, not the first | E-08 reply, RF-16 |
| A-07 | Online community (forums) | Strangers | Fast, often accurate answers on sequencing and documents | No follow-up, no accountability, mixed accuracy; one advice post suggested re-classifying non-covered items, which is misrepresentation | E-02, E-03, E-11 |

## Coverage by need

How each alternative handles the needs that recur across the evidence. Yes means the alternative does this as part of its normal service; Partly means sometimes or for one route; No means not at all.

| Need | A-01 Manual | A-02 Agent | A-03 Broker | A-04 Hospital desk | A-05 Chatbot | A-06 Legal | A-07 Forum |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Know the next step and its deadline | Partly | Partly | Partly | Partly | Partly | Yes | Partly |
| Record of what was sent, when, to whom | Partly | No | Unknown | No | No | Partly | No |
| Answer a repeated or renamed request with proof | Partly | No | Unknown | No | Partly | Yes | No |
| Plan claims across two covers | No | Partly | Unknown | No | Partly | No | Partly |
| Check a decision letter for missing reasons | Partly | No | Unknown | No | Partly | Yes | Partly |
| Escalate on time | Partly | No | Yes (vendor claim) | No | No | Yes | No |
| Confirm payment against the settlement | Partly | No | Unknown | No | No | No | No |
| Act on behalf of family with recorded authority | No | No | Unknown | No | No | Yes | No |
| Continues across the whole case | Yes, at high personal cost | No | Unknown | No | No | Only for the dispute | No |

This table is our interpretation of the evidence above, not a measured comparison. Unknown means public evidence was not enough to judge. Checking broker services at source is an open gap.

## What this means

- The manual workaround (A-01) already covers the whole lifecycle; its cost is effort, memory and timing, not missing information. That is the gap a lifecycle agent would have to close.
- Several alternatives are free and good at one step (A-04 at the hospital, A-07 for quick questions, A-05 for drafting). An agent that only does one of those steps is not worth building (reason 2 in the evidence register).
- Nobody in the comparison keeps a dated record of submissions and acknowledgements, or reconciles the settlement against the bank credit. Those are the clearest unmet needs, still to be validated (H-02, H-03).
