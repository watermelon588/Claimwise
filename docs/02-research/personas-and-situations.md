# Personas and situations

The class of users the evidence points to, and where in a claim each one gets stuck. Personas are composites drawn from several entries in the [evidence register](evidence-register.md), not portraits of individuals. Names are omitted on purpose.

## Personas

| ID | Persona | Who acts | Cover | Typical route | Authority situation | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U-1 | Patient handling their own claim | The policyholder | Retail or group policy, sometimes a second corporate cover | Reimbursement | Acts for themselves; no authority question | E-08, E-09, E-13 |
| U-2 | Adult child acting for a parent | Son or daughter, often working, sometimes in another city | Parent's retail policy, sometimes the child's corporate cover with parental co-pay | Reimbursement after cashless is denied or partial | Acts without recorded authority; the insurer may refuse to deal with them (S-27) | E-04, E-06, E-07 |
| U-3 | Parent acting for a minor | Parent | Family floater | Cashless with reimbursement for the remainder | Policyholder acting for a covered dependant | E-01 |
| U-4 | Household with two covers | Couple, or a family with retail plus employer policies | Two indemnity policies with different caps, sub-limits and co-pay | Reimbursement, claim order matters | The claimant chooses the primary insurer (RF-07) | E-02, E-03, E-04 |
| U-5 | Employee waiting on a cashless settlement | Policyholder | Corporate group policy through a TPA | Cashless, final settlement pending | Self | E-10 |
| U-6 | Family member abroad | Relative outside India coordinating care | Parent's policy | Any | Needs a registered authorized representative or a power of attorney; nominees have no rights while the policyholder is alive | S-27 (secondary, no firsthand case) |

The class these share: a person responsible for getting a hospital bill paid by one or more insurers, who may not be the policyholder, and who must keep the case moving for weeks or months while also dealing with illness.
