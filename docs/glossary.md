# Glossary

Terms used across the documents. Insurance terms follow their common Indian usage; where a regulation defines a term more precisely, the regulation prevails.

## Insurance terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Authorized representative | A person the insurer has recorded as allowed to act for the policyholder. Requirements vary by insurer. |
| Bima Bharosa | IRDAI's online portal for filing and tracking insurance complaints. |
| Cashless claim | The insurer pays a network hospital directly after pre-authorization; the patient pays only what is not covered. |
| Claims Review Committee (CRC) | The insurer committee whose approval is required before a claim is repudiated. |
| Co-payment (co-pay) | The fixed share of an admissible claim that the policyholder pays. |
| Contribution | How several indemnity policies share one claim so the total paid does not exceed the bill. |
| Day care treatment | Treatment needing less than 24 hours in hospital that a policy still covers as hospitalization. |
| Deductible | An amount the policyholder bears before the policy pays. |
| Discharge authorization | The insurer's final approval for a cashless claim at discharge. |
| Floater | One sum insured shared by several family members. |
| Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO) | The insurer's officer responsible for resolving complaints. |
| Group policy | A policy bought by an employer or institution for its members. |
| Indemnity policy | Pays actual expenses up to the sum insured. A benefit policy pays a fixed amount instead. |
| Insurance Ombudsman | An independent forum for insurance complaints after the insurer's grievance process, run through the Council for Insurance Ombudsmen (CIO). |
| Intimation | Telling the insurer that a claim is coming. Its date matters for timers and interest. |
| IRDAI | Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India. |
| Moratorium | After 60 months of continuous cover, a claim cannot be contested for non-disclosure except for established fraud. |
| NHCX | National Health Claims Exchange: a FHIR-based exchange for claim messages between insurers and hospitals. |
| No claim bonus (NCB) | An increase in cover or discount for a claim-free year. |
| Nominee | The person who receives benefits after the policyholder's death. Has no rights while the policyholder is alive. |
| Partial disallowance | Part of a claim refused, with the rest paid. |
| Portability | Moving a policy to another insurer with credit for waiting periods already served. |
| Power of attorney | A legal document letting one person act for another, made while the person is competent. |
| Pre-authorization | The insurer's approval before or at admission for a cashless claim. |
| Pre and post hospitalization | Expenses before admission and after discharge, covered for a set number of days. |
| RB-IOS | Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, for complaints against banks and payment providers. |
| Reimbursement claim | The patient pays the hospital, then claims the money back from the insurer. |
| Repudiation | Rejection of a claim. |
| Settlement letter | The insurer's statement of what was claimed, deducted and paid. |
| Sub-limit | A cap on a specific treatment or expense within the sum insured. |
| Sum insured | The maximum the policy pays in a policy year. |
| TAT | Turnaround time: the time allowed for an action. |
| TPA | Third Party Administrator: a company that processes claims for insurers. |

## Project terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Branch | A path a claim can take away from the standard journey, such as a repeated request or a partial settlement. |
| Checkpoint | One of the six lifecycle stages: before the event, during treatment, waiting, decision, after the decision, after resolution. |
| Gate | A review point where the project owner approves a step before the next one starts. |
| Manual handoff | A step a person must do in another system because no integration is available. |
| Partner-only | An integration available only to insurers, hospitals or approved partners. |
